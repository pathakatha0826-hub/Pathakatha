Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains the basic principles for handling information on PathaKatha.

Content & Image Disclaimer

The content published on PathaKatha is created to provide readers with useful information, inspiration, and background knowledge about travel destinations. To make our articles informative and engaging, textual information, facts, references, photographs, illustrations, maps, and other visual materials may be collected, referenced, or sourced from various publicly available sources, including official websites, public-domain resources, image platforms, and other online sources.

Information we collect

If you contact us, we may receive information such as your name, email address and the content of your message. We collect only information that is reasonably necessary to respond to you.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, improve the website, publish content and maintain website security. We do not intend to sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies or analytics tools in the future to understand website traffic and improve user experience. Any third-party service will be subject to its own privacy terms.

External links

PathaKatha may link to external websites and social platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.
